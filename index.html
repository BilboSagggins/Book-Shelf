Bring in your Goodreads library

  1. On goodreads.com (desktop site), open My Books, then Import and export in the left column.
  2. Choose Export Library. When the link appears, download the .csv file.
  3. Pick that file below. Read, Currently reading and Want to read map to your three shelves, with your star ratings.
Importing again later updates shelves and ratings for books that came from Goodreads, and keeps your notes. Books you added by hand are never duplicated.
The books marked “eg” are examples so you can see how the shelf works. Add your own, then clear them.

Tap a spine to take the book down.
